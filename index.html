Hand rankings

Highest to lowest:

  1. Royal Flush — A K Q J 10, one suit
  2. Straight Flush — five in sequence, one suit
  3. Four of a Kind — four cards, same rank
  4. Full House — three of a kind plus a pair
  5. Flush — five cards, one suit
  6. Straight — five in sequence, any suit
  7. Three of a Kind — three cards, same rank
  8. Two Pair — two different pairs
  9. One Pair — two cards, same rank
  10. High Card — highest single card

Game modes

Texas Hold'em: two hole cards, five community cards. Best five-card hand from seven.

Omaha Hi: four hole cards, must use exactly two. Best hand using two hole and three community cards.

5-Card Stud: one hole card, four face-up cards dealt one at a time with betting rounds.

5-Card Draw: five hole cards, one round to draw and discard, then final betting.

Blinds

Small blind is 10. Big blind is 20. The dealer button rotates every hand.
